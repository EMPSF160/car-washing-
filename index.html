The Science of Scratch-Free Care

How Nanotech
Protects Your Paint

Traditional car washes drag pressurized dirt particles across clear-coat paint, creating microscopic spider-web scratches. HydroShine uses high-lubricity polymers that chemically bond around dirt particles and lift them safely off the paint.

1

Encapsulate

Positively-charged polymers attract and coat each dirt microscopic grain.

2

Lift & Suspend

Creates a microscopic protective cushion between grime and clear coat.

3

Capture

Ultra-plush microfiber lifts the suspended dirt with zero scrubbing friction.

4

Ceramic Seal

Leaves an ultra-hydrophobic silicone barrier that repels road grime for weeks.
